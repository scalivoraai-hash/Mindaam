# Visit & Earn

Device location is a private verification input with explicit consent. A physical visit requires campaign eligibility plus short-lived merchant challenge/geofence checks. Browser GPS can be spoofed and must not be marketed as tamper-proof proof of presence.

Test location denial, inaccurate/stale fix, far-away claim, QR replay/expiry, incorrect merchant, own-business abuse, duplicate payout and campaign exhaustion. Never broadcast raw GPS in presence or analytics. Production fraud controls and retention policy require validation.
