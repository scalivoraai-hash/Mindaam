# Moderation

User reports and admin resolution require authorization and audit history. Blocks must prevent direct social/media contact and relevant discovery. Human triage, emergency handling and appeals require real assigned ownership.

Test reporter confidentiality, suspended user session revocation, blocked messaging/media, report abuse and audit immutability. Community rules are draft until approved. Automatic detection, coverage hours, appeals SLAs and emergency service integrations must not be advertised without implementation and operational evidence.
