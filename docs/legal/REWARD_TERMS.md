# DRAFT — Reward Terms

**For professional review. Not approved for publication, not certified legal advice, and not evidence of legal compliance.**

XP, Pulse Credits and Pulse Perks are separate. Rewards depend on server-validated eligibility, availability and published campaign conditions; no reward is guaranteed simply by viewing a screen or providing GPS.

Disclose expiry, restrictions and redemption conditions before participation. Credits do not have standard cash-out or arbitrary user transfer. Fraud investigations must use a review process; do not retrospectively remove earned value without a valid disclosed basis.

## Unresolved publication requirements

Complete purchased-credit cancellation/refund treatment, dormant balances, merchant failure, price changes and statutory-rights review. Verify any promotion/prize mechanic and avoid unreviewed gambling-like designs.
