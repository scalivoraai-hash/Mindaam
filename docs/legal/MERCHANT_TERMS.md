# DRAFT — Merchant Terms

**For professional review. Not approved for publication, not certified legal advice, and not evidence of legal compliance.**

Only an authorised representative may create or claim a business. Merchants must keep listings, prices and offer conditions accurate and honour valid Perks. Verification does not imply endorsement.

Campaign budgets, eligibility, reward amounts, cancellation, unused funding and settlement rules must be disclosed before payment. Fraudulent activity, misleading claims and requests for unnecessary personal data are prohibited.

## Unresolved publication requirements

Complete tax/invoicing, dispute/refund, advertising review, attribution limitations, controller/processor responsibilities, termination and liability terms. Reconcile these provisions to actual billing implementation.
