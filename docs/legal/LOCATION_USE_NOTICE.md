# DRAFT — Location Use Notice

**For professional review. Not approved for publication, not certified legal advice, and not evidence of legal compliance.**

Your avatar’s virtual location is separate from your device’s real location. Device location is optional and requested for a stated purpose such as checking eligibility for a merchant visit reward. Other players must not receive your precise device coordinates.

You may deny or revoke location permission; features requiring location verification may then be unavailable. Browser signals can be inaccurate. Do not travel into unsafe locations or use the app while driving.
