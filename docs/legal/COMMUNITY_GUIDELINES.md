# DRAFT — Community Guidelines

**For professional review. Not approved for publication, not certified legal advice, and not evidence of legal compliance.**

Do not harass, threaten, exploit, impersonate, discriminate, share non-consensual intimate material or reveal another person’s private location. Do not use the service while driving or enter unsafe/restricted property to obtain rewards.

Use block/report controls. Reports may lead to investigation and restrictions; moderators should apply proportionate decisions with an appeal route. Media permission is revocable and rewards never confer access to another person’s camera.

## Unresolved publication requirements

Add staffed support hours, appeals contact and response expectations. Do not imply continuous monitoring or emergency response; verify how urgent safety reports are handled.
