# Business tenancy

Business owner and admin checks protect mutable catalogue, offers, campaigns and analytics. Public business discovery must not reveal private owner, funding, payment or verification information. Merchant verification is an operator workflow.

Test a second unrelated business account against every read/write and analytics route. Verify denied users cannot infer sensitive record contents. Catalogue existence does not establish a full subscription/booking/advertising product; these remain scoped by release evidence.
