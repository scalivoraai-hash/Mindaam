# Analytics and cost controls

Derive counts from persisted events; online means current PULSEWORLD sessions, not city population. Separate virtual visit, claimed physical visit, verified eligible visit, redemption and paid conversion. Do not equate them.

Merchant queries require tenant authorization and suitable aggregation. Define retention, consent, event deduplication and attribution windows. Instrument provider consumption for tiles, media minutes/bandwidth, AI tokens, storage, DB, Redis and messaging; budget alerts are future operational configuration, not supplied automatically by Terraform.
